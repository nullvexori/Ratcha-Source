Privacy Policy

Mocha uses information to provide the game and RecNet, connect players to rooms and social features, process supported purchases and subscriptions, and help keep the community safe. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be shared, and how you can request deletion of your data.

The short version

We use your information to provide Mocha and RecNet, maintain accounts and game features, process supported transactions, and protect the community.

We do not sell your personal information. Public profile details and content you choose to make public can be seen by other players.

You can request deletion of your Mocha account and associated personal data at any time, regardless of where you live.

01

Information we collect

The information collected depends on which parts of Mocha you use.

  • Account and user information: your account ID, username, display name, linked platform and platform ID, password credentials, email address if you add one, profile image, banner, bio, level, balance, roles, account settings, and other information associated with your account.
  • Friends and social information: your friends, subscriptions or follows, blocked users, invites, parties, messages, social interactions, and related account relationships.
  • Profile and avatar information: profile details, avatar information, profile images, banners, and other customization information you choose to use.
  • Gameplay and community activity: rooms and instances you join, presence, challenges, events, progression, inventions, user-created content, reports, moderation actions, and other activity necessary to provide game and community features.
  • Purchases and subscriptions: information necessary to provide and manage supported in-app purchases, downloadable content, subscriptions, balances, entitlements, and related transaction records. Payment information handled directly by a third-party platform is not stored by Mocha unless specifically stated at the time of collection.
  • Device and technical information: device or platform identifiers provided by the game client, connection information, request times, error details, diagnostic information, security information, and server logs used to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the service.
  • Safety and enforcement information: information used to detect abuse, investigate reports, enforce community rules, prevent repeated abuse, and protect players. This may include device-related information used for device bans or other security enforcement.
  • Age-group information: age or age-group information provided by the platform or account system where necessary to provide age-appropriate features, restrictions, or experiences.
  • Content you provide: photos, comments, rooms, inventions, profile text, reports, messages, and other content you upload, submit, or create through Mocha.
02

How we use information

We use collected information only as reasonably necessary to operate and protect Mocha and its features. This includes:

  • creating, authenticating, and maintaining your account;
  • connecting you to rooms, instances, friends, parties, messages, invites, and other social features;
  • providing profiles, avatars, challenges, events, inventions, and other game features;
  • displaying public profiles and content that you choose to make public;
  • processing and maintaining supported purchases, downloadable content, subscriptions, balances, and entitlements;
  • maintaining friend, subscription, blocked-user, and other social relationships;
  • providing age-appropriate experiences and restrictions;
  • detecting abuse, fraud, cheating, and other prohibited activity;
  • investigating reports and enforcing community rules;
  • using device-related information when necessary for security enforcement, including device bans;
  • diagnosing errors, monitoring reliability, and improving the service; and
  • complying with applicable legal obligations and responding to valid legal requests.
03

When information is shared

Your public profile, public rooms, photos, comments, inventions, and other content that you choose to make public may be visible to other players.

Information required for social features may also be visible to the people involved in those features.

Private account information is accessed only by Mocha systems, authorized personnel, or service providers where reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or moderate the service.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, in response to a valid legal request, or when reasonably necessary to protect Mocha, its players, or another person.

Mocha does not sell your personal information.

04

Storage, retention, and security

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the service and maintain your account, or for legitimate purposes such as resolving disputes, enforcing community rules, preventing abuse, maintaining security, and meeting legal obligations.

When an account is deleted, Mocha will delete or de-identify personal information associated with the account where reasonably possible.

Limited information may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, moderation, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or system backups.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee perfect security.

05

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time

All Mocha users, regardless of geographic location, can request deletion of their personal data.

You can delete your account directly through RecNet Settings when the account deletion option is available. Account deletion permanently disables the account and begins the deletion process for personal information associated with it.

If you cannot access the account deletion option, you can request deletion by contacting Mocha through our official Discord community: discord.gg/mochapen .

When contacting the Mocha support or moderation team, clearly state that you are requesting deletion of your Mocha account and associated personal data.

When submitting a request, provide enough information for us to identify the account, such as your username or account ID. Never provide your password, authentication tokens, or other login credentials.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account before processing a deletion request. This verification is used to prevent unauthorized deletion of another user's account.

After a verified deletion request, we will delete or de-identify personal information that is no longer necessary for the operation of Mocha.

Some limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, moderation records, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or backups.

Retained information will not be used for unrelated purposes.

Requesting deletion does not require you to live in a particular country or region. The deletion mechanism described in this section is available to all Mocha users.

06

Junior accounts

Mocha may support junior accounts and junior-friendly rooms. Junior accounts may receive restricted communication and social features based on available account or platform information.

A parent or guardian may contact Mocha using the deletion or contact methods described in this policy if they need assistance regarding a child's information or wish to request deletion of information associated with the child's account.

We may require reasonable verification before providing account-specific information or processing a request.

07

Your choices

You can update most profile information from RecNet Settings .

You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information using the contact method described in the Data deletion and Contact sections.

Some requests may require verification that you own or control the account.

If information must be retained for security, moderation, legal, or other legitimate purposes, we will retain only what is reasonably necessary for that purpose.

08

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Mocha changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version is published.

Material changes may also be announced through RecNet or an in-game notice where appropriate.

09

Contact

For privacy questions, data access requests, correction requests, or deletion requests, contact Mocha through our official Discord community: discord.gg/mochapen .

When contacting us about an account, include your username or account ID when possible so that we can identify the relevant account. Never send your password, authentication tokens, or other login credentials.

Deletion requests are available to all Mocha users regardless of geographic location.
